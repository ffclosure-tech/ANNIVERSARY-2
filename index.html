THE LAST ORBIT

One year. One complete revolution.

Everything we were, everything we are — still here, still yours.

Made by Sheru, for Amna

12 November 2026
✦

Amna,

Happy one year anniversary. I planned this back in June — this website, and a few other things I wanted to keep as a surprise for you. It's been sitting in my head for months, waiting for today.

Things might not be at their best between us right now. We both have our differences, ones that have me — and maybe you too — holding our guards up. I'm not going to pretend that isn't true. But I want you to know, without any condition attached to it: I have always loved you. Your motherly kind of love for me, the way you took care of me, the way you worried about me — I've loved all of it. I've loved you, and I've loved what we had, and I've loved our little family more than anything else in my life. And I'm proud of that.

I'm only sad about the habits we couldn't change for each other in time. Not angry, not blaming — just sad. And I'm sorry for every little thing that ever hurt you, whether it came directly from me or from something I never even saw I was doing.

Because the truth is, across every universe and every timeline — in every version of this life where I get to exist — I love you more than I love myself. That part never changes. Not in this one, not in any of them.

Maybe right now the differences are louder than the love. Maybe we need distance, silence, space to breathe. I don't know what comes after this — but I know what came before it was the best year of my life, and I know I'd choose it again.

Thank you for one whole year of being my universe.

— Always yours, Sheru
